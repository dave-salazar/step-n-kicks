Shipping & returns

Delivery

  • We deliver anywhere in the Philippines.
  • Standard delivery usually takes 3–6 days after your order is confirmed. Remote areas may take longer.
  • Shipping is ₱150, and free for orders of ₱5,000 or more.
  • You'll see the final total before you place the order.

Payment

We currently accept cash on delivery. Please prepare the exact amount for the courier. GCash and Maya are coming soon.

Order status and cancellation

Track your order under My orders. You can cancel an order yourself while it is still "Order placed". After that, contact us as soon as possible.

Returns

  • You can return a pair within 7 days of receiving it.
  • The pair must be unworn, in its original box and in the condition you received it.
  • If you received the wrong item or a defective pair, we cover the return shipping.
  • To start a return, contact us with your order number.
  • Approved returns are refunded to your original payment method or as agreed with you.
